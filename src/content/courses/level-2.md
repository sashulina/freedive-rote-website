Level 2 is designed for freedivers who want to dive deeper into the world of freediving, improve technique and efficiency, and learn the skills needed to train independently.

The course introduces freefall — the point at which you begin to sink on the descent, falling effortlessly into the blue.

It also covers advanced equalisation, deeper diving techniques and the essential safety protocols that come with them.
