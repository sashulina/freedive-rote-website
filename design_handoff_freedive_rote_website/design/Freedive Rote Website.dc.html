Why train with us

Whether you’re interested in freediving or looking to feel more confident in the surf, we’ll meet you where you are and help you progress at your own pace.

  • Increasing breath-holding capability

  • Understanding O₂, CO₂ and the urge to breathe

  • Managing high-risk surf scenarios

Who we are

We are surfers, freedivers and ocean people, probably much like yourself.

Freedive Rote is a beach-based ocean centre in Nemberala, Rote Island, built around a simple idea: helping people feel more calm, confident and connected in the water.

Our connection to the ocean has been shaped by many years spent surfing and sailing throughout Indonesia and Papua. Freediving became a natural extension of that relationship, and for more than 15 years we have been coaching, teaching and exploring the underwater world.

Our journey

Our journey includes co-founding Apneista Freediving & Yoga in Amed, Bali, along with Above Below, an expedition company running freediving trips and liveaboards to Raja Ampat, the Banda Sea, North Sumatra, Komodo, Tonga and Baja Mexico.

For surfers

More than teaching freediving

Freedive Rote is not just about teaching freediving. As surfers ourselves, we know how much the skills and awareness developed through freediving can help build confidence in the surf.

We offer surf survival, breath-hold training, ocean awareness and nervous system regulation to help surfers stay calmer, feel more prepared and build confidence when things get challenging, whether that is a hold down, a bigger wave or simply learning to stay relaxed under pressure.

Surf survival
Breath, awareness, presence

Meditation and mindfulness are also central to our approach. A long standing Vipassana practice has shaped the way we teach, with an emphasis on breath, awareness, relaxation and the connection between body and mind.

For us, freediving is not simply about going deeper or holding your breath longer. It is about learning to become calm, present and comfortable in the ocean.

Stillness in motion.

Whether you are learning to freedive, looking to improve your surf survival skills, or simply wanting to feel more relaxed and at home in the water, we combine 15+ years of coaching experience, practical ocean knowledge, modern training techniques and a mindful approach to help you progress at your own pace.

Rote Island & Nemberala

Freedive Rote is located right on the shores of Nemberala Beach, in the village of Oenggaut on Rote Island.

Rote lies at the southern edge of Indonesia, known for its pristine beaches, world class surf, beautiful reefs and abundant marine life.

It is a place where life still moves at a slower pace, far removed from the hustle and bustle of Bali.

Our school

Our school sits directly on the beach, with our training taking place in a beachside shala.

Water sessions begin in the calm, shallow lagoon before progressing behind the waves for deeper water sessions as students develop their skills and confidence.

Beyond the waves

Discover Rote

Rote is still something of a hidden gem. While the island is famous among surfers for Besialu / T-Land, the long left hander that draws surfers from around the world during the main surf season from June to September, there is much more to Rote than its waves.

The island offers a chance to slow down and experience a simpler way of life. Explore quiet beaches and coral reefs, paddle through mangrove lagoons, discover traditional Ikat weaving, encounter the island’s famous wild horses, or simply enjoy the small collection of cafes and restaurants around Nemberala.

Goats wander through the villages, while the landscape shifts between dry savannah and palm fringed coastal areas.

Oh, and the sunsets over the ocean are spectacular!

Freedive Rote

With direct beach access, calm sheltered water and a peaceful environment, Freedive Rote is an ideal place to learn, slow down and connect with the ocean.

Whether you are here to learn freediving, improve your surf survival skills, explore the reefs or simply spend some time in the water, Rote offers the space to breathe, reset and reconnect.
